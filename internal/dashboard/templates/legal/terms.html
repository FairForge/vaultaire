Service Description

stored.ge provides S3-compatible object storage. You can store, retrieve, and manage objects (files) via our API, dashboard, or any S3-compatible client. The service includes:

  • S3-compatible API for object operations (GET, PUT, DELETE, LIST, HEAD).
  • Web dashboard for account management, bucket configuration, and usage monitoring.
  • API key management with scoped access control.
  • CDN delivery for public buckets.

Usage Limits

Storage is sold as a quota in whole terabytes at a flat per-terabyte rate (the free tier is 5 GB). Your quota and egress allowance are displayed on your dashboard. There are no overage charges:

  • A write that would exceed your quota is refused with a quota error until you resize the quota (up or down, prorated) or delete data. Existing data stays readable.
  • Egress is never billed. Past the monthly allowance keyed to your quota, download throughput may be throttled; archive restores beyond their allowance are queued.
  • We may rate-limit management API requests (100 per minute per account).
  • We will notify you before taking any action that affects your ability to access stored data.
