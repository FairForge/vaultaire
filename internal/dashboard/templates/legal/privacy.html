Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases as defined by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Performance of a contract — processing necessary to provide the storage service you signed up for, including account management, billing, and service delivery.
  • Legitimate interest — processing necessary for service security, fraud prevention, abuse detection, and service improvement, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.

Data Categories

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data — email address, company name, hashed password, account creation date, email verification status.
  • Usage data — storage consumption, bandwidth usage, API request counts, bucket and object metadata (names, sizes, content types, ETags). We do not read or analyse the content of your stored objects; our engine handles object bytes only to store them (chunking, deduplication, compression) and returns them unchanged.
  • Billing data — subscription plan, payment method (processed by Stripe; we do not store card numbers), invoice history, Stripe customer ID.
  • Session data — IP address, user agent, session creation and last-active timestamps, used for active session management and security.

Important: We never read, analyze or profile the content of objects you store in our service. Object content is encrypted in transit (TLS) and treated as opaque data.

Retention Periods

  • Account data — retained for the lifetime of your account. A deletion request starts a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel; on the date the account is closed, the subscription is cancelled and our deletion job erases your objects, keys and account records (see the Terms); database backups age out within 7 days of the erasure.
  • Session records — expire 24 hours after sign-in and are purged hourly. S3 access logs — retained 30 days, then automatically purged (request-level logs are only kept for buckets whose owner has enabled server access logging; error records are kept for every bucket for support, for the same 30 days). API/dashboard event logs, quota usage events and payment-provider event records — retained 90 days, then automatically purged. Public-link (CDN) access logs — aggregated into daily counts and the raw entries purged after 2 days. Webhook delivery records — retained 30 days. Sign-in and administrative audit records — retained for the life of the service as our security record; your own entries are removed with your account. Waitlist sign-up IP address and user agent — removed 90 days after sign-up (the address itself is kept until launch or your request).
  • Billing records — retained for 7 years to comply with financial reporting and tax obligations.
  • Object metadata — deleted when you delete the corresponding object (version records on versioning-enabled buckets remain until the bucket is deleted), and with the account records after account deletion.

Data Subject Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON export).
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your account and all associated personal data, subject to a 30-day grace period.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate personal data (e.g., update your email or company name via account settings).
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@stored.ge. We will respond within 30 days.

Sub-processors

We use the following third-party sub-processors to deliver our service:

  • iDrive Inc. (United States; EU regions on request) — primary object storage for Standard-tier objects, in the region you choose per bucket.
  • Seagate Technology (Lyve Cloud) (United States) — object storage for the resilient tier and as a failover leg.
  • Geyser Data Inc. (United States, Los Angeles) — tape archive for Vault-tier objects (GLACIER / DEEP_ARCHIVE), whatever the bucket's region.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (global edge) — CDN, DDoS protection and TLS termination; Cloudflare R2 holds the objects of public-read buckets served on cdn.stored.ge.
  • Microsoft Corporation (OneDrive) (United States) — internal parity copies of objects, when enabled.
  • Stripe Inc. (United States) — payment processing and subscription management.
  • Resend Inc. (United States) — transactional e-mail, when enabled.
  • Google LLC / GitHub Inc. (United States) — sign-in with Google or GitHub, only if you choose it (we receive your verified e-mail address).

Each sub-processor is bound by a Data Processing Agreement that requires GDPR-equivalent protections.

International Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We ensure adequate protection for international transfers through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.
  • Sub-processor agreements that incorporate GDPR-equivalent data protection terms.

Security Measures

  • All data in transit is protected by TLS 1.2 or higher; HSTS is enforced at our edge.
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (cost factor 10, the Go library default).
  • Sessions use cryptographically random tokens with HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite cookies.
  • Two-factor authentication (TOTP) is available for all accounts.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel with SSH key authentication.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users at least 30 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
