Customer key

On, every upload into this bucket must bring its own AES-256 key (the SSE-C headers); an upload without one is refused and nothing is stored. We never keep the key: it passes through our servers on each request, and names, sizes, timestamps and access logs stay readable to us. It can be turned on only while the bucket holds nothing we can read: every file in it written with your key, and no upload without one in progress — otherwise create a new bucket that requires your key and copy the data into it. A public bucket cannot require a key, and a bucket that does cannot receive access logs or inventory reports.
