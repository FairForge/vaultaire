## Verdicts and the store's queue (Prompt 25 Part 0, RP30-1…6)
BTCPay 2.4.5 sends one delivery at a time per webhook (`MultiProcessingQueue` keyed by webhook id), on an `HttpClient` with no timeout (100 s), and its automatic redelivery waits 10 s, 1 min, 6 × 10 min **inside the queued task**: one 5xx stalls every other invoice of the store for ~71 min. So the handler answers 5xx only for what a retry can fix (DB error, Greenfield 5xx / no answer, the lock not free in 15 s). Every delivery that applies nothing is a **verdict** (`applied.verdict`): 200, the claim **rolled back** (a redelivery — same `originalDeliveryId` — is handled fresh, RP30-3), counted under its outcome: `foreign` (not ours, unknown at BTCPay, metadata/amount mismatch — Warn; rule `BTCPayWebhookForeign` warns), `settled_unpaid`, `mark_refused`, `fetch_refused` (Error; rule `BTCPayPaymentNotApplied` pages). `IsPermanent(err)` (`client.go`) = a 4xx other than 408/429. A second webhook on the store has its own queue: overlapping deliveries are serialised by the lock (RP30-1) — the store keeps ONE webhook, ever. Tests `wp_p25_part0_test.go`: `TestBTCPayWebhook_TwoSettleDeliveriesOverlap_GrantOnce`, `_ExpiredAfterConcurrentSettle_NoCredit`, `_ForeignThenRedeliveredAfterFix_Applies`, `_MarkStatus422_200Counted`, `_FetchRefused_200Counted`, `_SettledUnpaid_Counted`, `_NewOutcomesExistAtZero`; `config_test.go` `TestLoadConfig_PlainHTTPOnlyToLoopback`.

## Switching providers (WP-PAY-5, Prompt 25)
- **Implicit switch (RP30-2):** `applyOrder` (under the lock, after Part 0's re-read) reads the row it replaces: another provider's house with a reference that renews `auto` (or any Stripe reference) is written into a **`renewal_stop` row in the claim's transaction** — pending (`implicit_switch`) when it renewed by itself, stopped (`switch`) when the customer had asked for the switch (`SwitchToPrepaid` stopped it then). The BTC period is appended from `paid_through` as before; the grant names btcpay/prepaid. After the commit: `billing.provider_switched` {from, to, previous_ref, invoice, implicit}, and for an implicit one the stop is asked at once through the registry (`SetRenewalStopper` — `payments.Registry.StopPendingRenewals`, a detached 15 s context, best effort); the `renewal_stops` job (10 min) retries. Decision: the Stripe call is **after** the commit with a durable row, never before the grant — a Stripe outage can never turn a delivery into a 5xx that stalls the store's queue (RP30-4).
- **`InvalidateOpenInvoices(tenant, reason)`** (`payments.InvoiceInvalidator`, wired into the Stripe webhook by `api/server.go`): every pending/processing invoice of the tenant is FETCHED and marked Invalid only when **provably unpaid** — `unpaidForInvalid`: New or Expired with `paidAmount` 0 (RP31-1, Stage 1a Prompt 26: Processing is paid; BTCPay 2.4.5 would let us mark it, `CanMarkInvalid` = New|Processing|Expired — the rule is ours). One holding money is left to settle (its settle is an implicit switch) and counted `outcome="invalidate_skipped_paid"` (0 from init) with a Warn; a fetch that fails marks nothing. Each mark audited `billing.invoice_marked` {reason}; a 4xx refusal = Error + `mark_refused`, never the caller's error.
- **`CancelUnpaidInvoice(tenant, invoice, actor)`** (`payments.InvoiceCanceller`, RP31-5): the customer's way out of the `crypto_invoice_open` 409 — our open row must be the tenant's (`payments.OpenInvoiceOf`, else `ErrNoSuchPayment`), the invoice provably unpaid (else the `*OpenInvoiceError` with `Paid` set = `ErrInvoicePaid`), then `MarkStatus(Invalid)` (reason `customer_cancel`) and our row closed (`invalid`) under the entitlement lock at once, so the card checkout's guard passes without waiting for BTCPay's delivery.
- **The Invalid state credits** (RP31-1 b): a fetched `Invalid` on a row that never settled is `Invalidate` + `SetCredit(partial:<invoice>, paidAmount)` — money that reached an Invalid invoice (before a mark, or after it) is the tenant's credit, never lost; a settled row's Invalid credits nothing (the grant stands).
- The double-period detector's report runs in `applied.after` (`payments.ReportDoublePeriod` with the receipt mailer as the operator notifier) for every settle — it also audits a designed forfeit (`Settled.Forfeit`, RP31-2).
