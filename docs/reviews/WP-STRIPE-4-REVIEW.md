## Verdict

**HOLDS at P2.** The shape is right:
- the role is read from the row on every request, never from the claim (D-STRIPE-32);
- `auth.Can` is one table, and both router walks (management and dashboard) fail a write that names no action;
- the owner is permanent, and no invitation makes an owner;
- an invitation token is 32 random bytes with only its SHA-256 stored, spent once under `FOR UPDATE`, and bound to the addressee;
- the switcher re-checks membership and rotates the session;
- the audit tenant is stamped by `requireMember`, never by `requireJWT` (D-STRIPE-39), and the operator-trail scrub keeps a member's rows about other tenants when that member is erased.

All 20 tests the note claims exist by name.

One P2, three P3s and two P4s. The P2 and the first P3 come from the same change: PR C made `api_keys.user_id` mean "the member who made the key" (D-STRIPE-40), and two older rules still read it as "the tenant's owner".
