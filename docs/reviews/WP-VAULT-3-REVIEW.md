## Cleared

- **Stale-etag P0** (the one that matters): `Open` and `CanServe` refuse unless the row is `complete` for the GET's cached etag AND size; shards live under `<digest>/<etag>/`; a same-etag re-protect rewrites byte-identical shards (RS is deterministic), so `rangeIdentity` never splices and a stale folder erased mid-read fails cleanly. `errRangeIdentity` fails the WHOLE read; the body is cut with `Content-Length` already set → the connection closes on the short write, never a 200 with wrong bytes. A client cancel never charges a leg; `settle` excludes EOF, `errRangeIdentity`, and an undelivered window says nothing.
- **Budget:** parfetch owns its ranged parts; the ring's stripes and the reservation are released in `aheadShardReader.Close` under `sync.Once`; every early exit settles and closes; `Budget` nil-safe; `s.vaultParity` built before `LargeGet` is set.
- **#1117:** both Lyve write paths are seekable (the manager's buffers; a single PUT and each part tested); a presign has no stream; the put-deadline retry re-runs the Build stack; an existing `Content-MD5` (an edge presign) passes untouched.
- **#1115:** `sg` is a jurisdiction of its own — a Singapore-pinned bucket admits no US leg (single copy, RP76-1's rule) and no residency admits it; the picker and the GDPR lists are table-driven. Edge writes without a client MD5 are already `admitted_unverified` (D-EDGE-30) and the commit refuses a non-MD5 ETag — the switch changed nothing there.
