## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-P25-1 | **RP30-1:** the tenant is resolved from our row by a plain read (a row's tenant never changes); then `payments.Lock` (waited ≤ 15 s, `webhookLockWait`), then the row again **FOR UPDATE** (`payments.LockedPending`), then the invoice and its payment methods are fetched, then applied. The Expired branch acts only while the row is `Opened.Open()` (pending / processing / expired*) — never on settled / refunded. **Cost, said plainly:** the entitlement lock is now held across two Greenfield GETs (≤ 10 s each, one retry on a fresh connection for a GET with no answer) — as the checkout already holds it across `CreateInvoice`. | Everything that decides a write is read under the lock: the second of two overlapping deliveries sees what the first committed. The 15 s wait is well inside BTCPay's 100 s and makes "a checkout holding the lock" a wait, not a 5xx (RP30-4). |
| D-P25-2 | **RP30-3: a verdict that applied nothing rolls its claim back** and answers 200 (`applied.verdict`): foreign, unknown at BTCPay, our invoice gone, another store, metadata / amount mismatch, Settled-unpaid, a refused mark or read. Chosen over "keep the row and re-handle a redelivery of a non-applied claim". | No schema, one rule, and BTCPay's redelivery — automatic or from the UI after the fix (same `originalDeliveryId`) — is simply a fresh event. The body is still in the Warn/Error line; `InvoiceCreated` and a stale event on a settled row are handled no-ops and keep their claim. |
| D-P25-3 | **RP30-4:** `btcpay.IsPermanent(err)` = a Greenfield 4xx other than 408/429. `MarkStatus` refused → 200 `mark_refused`; a read refused with a 4xx other than "no such invoice" (a 401 wrong key) → 200 `fetch_refused`; both Error lines, both claims released. 5xx only for a DB error, a Greenfield 5xx / no answer, the lock not free. | One 5xx stalls every other invoice of the store for ~71 min (`WebhookSender.cs`: the redelivery waits run inside the queued task). A permanent refusal needs a person; the 200 + page + released claim lets the UI redelivery apply after the fix. |
| D-P25-4 | **RP30-5:** outcomes `settled_unpaid` / `mark_refused` / `fetch_refused` on `vaultaire_payment_webhook_total{provider="btcpay"}` (0 from `init()`); **BTCPayPaymentNotApplied** (critical, any increase of the three in 1 h), **BTCPayWebhookForeign** (warning). | Nothing retries a 200: a person must look. |
| D-P25-5 | **RP30-6:** `BTCPAY_URL` plain http only to localhost / a loopback IP (`ErrInsecureURL`): one boot Error naming `BTCPAY_URL`, not registered, no route — never a refused boot. | The store key is in every request header (the Blomp lesson); a misconfiguration is not a reason to take the whole service down. |
| D-P25-6 | **RP30-7:** `RecordRefund(FromProvider)` hitting a refund already recorded under its id with another amount = `ErrRefundMismatch`; the webhook counts `vaultaire_payments_total{stripe,refund,mismatch}` (0 from init), Error, acknowledged; **StripeRefundMismatch** (warning). The operator's row is kept. | The operator who typed the amount first corrects it; Stripe's notice never silently confirms it. |
| D-P25-7 | **Item 0 (RP30-2): the implicit switch is stopped AFTER the commit, with a durable row** — not before the grant with a bounded call. The settle appends the BTC period, names the house btcpay/prepaid and writes a `renewal_stop` row (`payment_events`, status pending, reason `implicit_switch`) in the claim's transaction; after the commit the registry asks Stripe (`StopRenewalRef`: read first — gone / already stopping = done — then `cancel_at_period_end=true`, never a cancel), and the **`renewal_stops`** job (10 min) retries until it is confirmed. | A Stripe outage must never turn a BTCPay delivery into a 5xx that stalls the store's queue (RP30-4), and the money that arrived must be applied now. The row is what keeps the subscription findable until then. |
| D-P25-8 | **The `renewal_stop` row is what names a switched-away subscription** — for the deletion runner (cancels every non-gone one; pending = renews by itself, so an unconfigured Stripe defers the tenant) and for the Stripe webhook (`switchedAway`: a granting event of it is never applied; an un-cancel re-opens the row and asserts the stop at once, `billing.renewal_stop_reasserted`). **Departure from the prompt's "findable by isCurrentSubscription":** `isCurrentSubscription` stays the entitlement's ref. | Its clearing events (canceled, deleted) must NOT clear the house that replaced it — "stale" is the right verdict for them. What must not happen is a GRANT from it, and that is what `switchedAway` stops. |
| D-P25-9 | **A switch the customer asked for** (`SwitchToPrepaid`) calls `StopRenewal` synchronously under the lock (a Stripe refusal writes nothing) and keeps the entitlement on Stripe (prepaid, `pending_provider`). When its bitcoin invoice settles, the replaced Stripe ref is recorded `stopped` (reason `switch`). | The customer is at the page and can be told; no row is needed until the entitlement stops naming the subscription. |
| D-P25-10 | **§ 4.1 step 5 departure:** an un-cancel of a subscription whose switch already SETTLED re-asserts the stop (prevention) — the detector stays as the backstop if Stripe bills anyway. An un-cancel BEFORE the bitcoin invoice settled restores `auto`, withdraws the switch (`pending_provider` cleared in the grant's transaction) and marks open BTCPay invoices Invalid (`portal_uncancel`, audited; a refused mark = `mark_refused`, never a 5xx). | The period was paid in bitcoin; billing it again by card is the double charge the detector would only report. |
| D-P25-11 | **The detector checks both bounds** (`other.end > this.start AND other.start < this.end`) and ignores $0 rows on either side. Counted `{provider}` of the later charge; audit `billing.double_period` names both rows; `Mailer.NotifyOperator` → the support address; the grant stands. Fires from the Stripe webhook, the BTCPay webhook and `RecordManualPayment`. | The design's one-sided `period_end > period_start` flags a later, adjacent prepaid period; a trialing subscription's $0 first invoice (Switch to card) paid nothing twice. |
| D-P25-12 | **Bulk = a manual job** (`jobSpec.Manual`: never scheduled, the generic `/jobs/{job}/run` refuses it; `StartDetachedWith` runs the action asked): the `job_runs` row, lock and counters of every job; dry run by default. **Resume departure from § 4.3:** only rows whose last renewal change was the bulk stop (audit `reason = bulk`). | The admin_jobs.go pattern for 202/409; a customer who stopped their own renewal is never resumed by the owner. |
| D-P25-13 | **409 `crypto_invoice_open`** for a provider whose `Renewal()` is `auto` while a charge of another provider is pending/processing or expired inside `PrepaidInvoiceWindow` (24 h = BTCPay's `monitoringMinutes`). | A page that says "pay it, or wait until <date>" is better than an implicit switch after the fact. |
