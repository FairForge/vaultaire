## What is wrong, measured

1. **Placement is `hash(full path) % slots`** (`onedrive.go:331` `homeTenantIndex`, `:346` `tenantsFor`). Appending the fourth drive remaps about three quarters of every object's home; losing one remaps the rest. RB3 (#1185) made that *safe* — the marker's `placed_under` keeps the fleet walk on until a rebalance rewrites it — but not *cheap*: there is no rebalance tool, and the walk is capped at `odFallbackWalkMax` = 8 accounts (`onedrive_scale.go:67`), so past eight drives a remapped object is unreachable until it is moved.
2. **The four parity shards of one object land wherever their names hash** — on 2026-11-06's soak4 that put two shards' worth of traffic on one tenant, and the 400 GB/h per-app-per-tenant budget ("no license bound", Microsoft Learn 2026-08-10) bit first: 12 of 21 rounds lost reads. A rebuild of one object is capped at one tenant's ~111 MB/s, and a lost tenant can take two of an object's four shards.
3. **`List`, `ListDir` and `RemoveEmptyDir` are the fleet's union** (`onedrive.go:760`, `:895`, `:927`) because no drive can be named for a folder (D-F11-4, `onedrive_scale.go:37`). The parity reconcile walks `<tenant>__parity/<digest>/<etag>/` through `ListDir` (`internal/api/vault_parity_reconcile.go:380–393`) within 1,000 listings per leg per run: at 40 drives every folder listing is 40 Graph calls.
4. **The tenant is invisible to placement.** `odTenant` (`onedrive.go:154`) is an *account* (one drive); WP-FLEET-11 grouped accounts under a tenant for credentials and transports (`onedrive_scale.go`, D-F11-2) but placement never learned it. The sizing rule — 8 to 16 five-TB users per tenant, ≥ 4 tenants (`docs/FLEET.md` § Sizing a tenant) — needs placement to know which drives share a pipe.

## Decisions (made — do not reopen)

- **D-F5-1 Placement from the key alone, in two steps.** A key's **group** is its parent folder (`path.Dir`); its **shard index** *j* is the integer of a leaf matching `^p(\d+)$` (the parity layout `<tenant>__parity/<digest>/<etag>/p<j>`), else 0. The tenants stand in **columns**: tenant *k* — its position in the fleet's order of first appearance, which is append-only — is in column *k* mod `PERMAFROST_PLACEMENT_SPREAD` (default 4). Step one: shard *j* takes a tenant of column *j* mod spread by rendezvous (HRW: `h = hash64(tenantKey ‖ group)`, highest wins); an empty column (fewer tenants than the spread) falls back to every tenant, each holding at most ⌈max(spread, j+1)/n⌉ shards of the group. Step two: the **drive** inside that tenant by rendezvous on `(driveKey ‖ group)` — the group alone, so every file of one group that lands on a tenant lands on ONE drive of it. Consequences, all pinned by tests on a fake fleet: shards 0..3 of one object sit on four distinct tenants when there are ≥ 4 (⌈4/n⌉ per tenant at fewer); every file of one group on one tenant sits on ONE drive of it; **adding a tenant fills one column and moves about one shard in (its size + 1) of that column's — an eighth of all shards on a 4 → 5 step, a twelfth on 8 → 9**; adding a drive to a tenant moves ~1/d of THAT tenant's keys and nothing elsewhere; nothing moves when nothing changes. The first design tried — each shard ranking the tenants and excluding the ones earlier shards took — **moved half of every shard on 4 → 5** (measured in PR A's red run): with as many tenants as shards the assignment is forced and every change cascades; a ranking shared across shards does the same. Columns are key-only and insertion-stable; their price is balance when the tenant count is not a multiple of the spread (5 tenants at spread 4: three hold a quarter, two an eighth — pinned, and the reason `docs/FLEET.md` § Sizing a tenant says to keep the count a multiple of the spread). Hash: FNV-1a 64 over the identity strings (the marker's existing hashes), never a secret. The tenant key is a hash of the directory id; the drive key is the account identity the marker already hashes.
- **D-F5-2 The marker records the placement.** `fleetMarker` (`onedrive_fleet.go:74`) gains `placement_version` (`json:"placement_version,omitempty"`; absent = 1 = `hash % slots` over the recorded `fleet` order; 2 = D-F5-1) and the fleet's **tenant grouping** as written (`tenants: [{id-hash, drives: [index…]}]`), so a reader can compute the OLD placement exactly. `placed_under` keeps its meaning (the order the data is laid out for). `authoritative()` (`onedrive_scale.go:88`) is true only when `placed_under` matches AND `placement_version` is the current one on every marker.
- **D-F5-3 The fallback is the previous placement, computed — never a walk.** While not authoritative, a miss at the current home asks exactly one more place: the home under the placement the markers record (version 1 over `fleet`, or version 2 over the recorded tenants/drives). Two requests, whatever the fleet size; `odFallbackWalkMax` and the capped walk go (the metric `vaultaire_permafrost_fallback_walks_total{op}` stays, now counting second looks). A marker without `placed_under`/`placement_version` (prod's, written before) means version 1 over its own `fleet`, which is what prod has today — nothing rewritten, prod authoritative until the first growth.
- **D-F5-4 Listing by placement where placement can name the drives.** `ListDir(container, dir)` of a **group folder** (one whose files are leaves — the reconcile's `<digest>/<etag>` level, and any folder the caller names fully) asks the group's spread set: the first `PERMAFROST_PLACEMENT_SPREAD` ranked tenants (default 4; ≥ the largest shard index in use + 1), one drive each by D-F5-1 with *j* = 0..spread−1. `RemoveEmptyDir` deletes the folder on the same set after each lists it empty. `ListDir` of a folder whose children are folders (the container root, the `<digest>` level) and `List(container, prefix)` (the erasure sweep) stay the union — one listing per DRIVE, as today; WP-FLEET-9 (`/delta`, `$batch`) owns their cost. Pinned: a reconcile over a fake 12-drive fleet with a 4-tenant spread lists each etag folder with 4 calls, not 12.
- **D-F5-5 `cmd/tools/permafrost-rebalance`** (the shape of `permafrost-migrate-drive`, #1169): reads the fleet (env slots or `PERMAFROST_FLEET_FILE`) and every marker; `-dry-run` (default) prints per account what moves where and how many bytes; `-run` moves each file whose current home ≠ its target: same tenant → Graph cross-drive `copy` (monitored to completion); other tenant → ranged download + upload session; **every copy verified by quickXorHash against the source's**, then `permanentDelete` of the source; resumable (a target that already exists with the right hash is skipped; a source already gone is skipped); paced through the driver's own buckets (`PERMAFROST_ACCOUNT_*`, `PERMAFROST_TENANT_GBH`) so a rebalance is a good tenant too; `-concurrency` (default 4). **Markers last:** only when every account reports nothing left to move does `-commit` rewrite every marker with `placed_under` = the current digest and `placement_version` = 2 — the moment reads become authoritative. A run cut at any point leaves the fleet readable (D-F5-3). Never deletes anything it did not just copy and verify.
- **D-F5-6 `PERMAFROST_FLEET_ACCEPT` keeps its meaning** (RB3): it accepts an order; it never claims placement. The README procedure becomes accept → `permafrost-rebalance -dry-run` → `-run` → `-commit` → `PermafrostFallbackWalking` falls to 0.
- **D-F5-7 Shard *j* comes from the key, not from the parity job.** No new `engine.PutOption`; `vault_parity.go`'s layout is unchanged. A future layout that wants spread names its leaves `p<j>`.
- **D-F5-8 Metrics**: `vaultaire_permafrost_placement_version` (gauge: the version reads are authoritative under, 0 while unplaced), `vaultaire_permafrost_fallback_walks_total{op}` (kept), `vaultaire_permafrost_rebalance_files_total{outcome=moved|skipped|failed}` (the tool's, printed, not scraped). Rule `PermafrostFallbackWalking` unchanged.
- **D-F5-9 No change** to pacing, transfers, delete semantics, the fleet file format beyond the marker, or the parity codec. Spread > 4 (RS 4+5, a fifth tenant) is a parity-job decision for later; the driver already handles any *j*.
