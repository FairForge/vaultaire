## Memory (RSS) finding — transient, not a leak

RSS during the run: 41 MB baseline → **6.24 GB peak** during the Multipart
scenario → pinned ~5.8 GB for a few minutes → **166 MB** once Go's periodic
GC + scavenger ran (~5 min after the burst). Box has 126 GB RAM; peak = 5%.

Cause: each assembled 100 MB multipart object exceeds the 64 MB chunking
threshold and enters the chunked-PUT path, which bounds in-flight chunk
buffers **per upload** (8 workers + chunker buffer ≤ ~19 × 16 MB ≈ 300 MB)
but has **no cap across concurrent uploads** — 50 simultaneous completes ≈
6 GB in flight. This is precisely the "memory cap" item deferred from the
#400 perf arc, now quantified.

**Risk assessment:** not launch-blocking at current scale (126 GB RAM;
worst case needs ~400 concurrent >64 MB PUTs to reach 50% RAM). The
deferred WP stands: a global semaphore on total in-flight chunk-store
bytes. Until then `CHUNK_PUT_CONCURRENCY` is the blunt lever.
